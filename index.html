What is the difference between liability and comprehensive coverage?
AI
Liability coverage pays for damage you cause to others — their car, property, or medical bills — when you're at fault. Comprehensive coverage protects your own vehicle from non-collision events like theft, fire, weather damage, or hitting an animal. Most lenders require both if you're financing a vehicle.
